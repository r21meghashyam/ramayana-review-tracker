How these counts work. One reviewer is counted at most once per sentiment per topic. A mixed review can count in both positive and negative, but never in gray. Gray counts the reviewers who expressed neither sentiment on that topic. Because a mixed opinion appears twice, the three colored segments represent recorded responses rather than 27 exclusive people. “Brahma AI lip sync” covers altered mouth movements. “English dubbing” covers voice performances or an expressed preference against the dubbed version. A reviewer can discuss both. “Songs” counts opinions on the songs themselves; comments that songs were removed from the shorter English cut appear in the separate note above. The counts reflect this limited sample of reviews and firsthand reactions through 5 November 2026; they are not an audience poll. Venky Reviews is excluded because their viewing circumstances could not be confirmed. Reviews cover both the shorter English cut and Indian release versions, which can differ. Most YouTube transcripts were unavailable, so video-only claims are limited to the creators’ descriptions and corroborated reporting.
